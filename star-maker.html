    Star·Maker

    Hold your phone up to the night sky. Star Maker looks through your camera, works out which way you're pointing from your compass and location, and draws the constellations right where they really are.

    Tap any constellation to hear its story — and what it could mean for you.

    Camera, compass, and location are asked for only when you tap Start, and they're used only on this device to aim the sky — nothing is sent anywhere. No camera or compass? The demo sky still works: drag to look around.
