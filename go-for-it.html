How it would work on game day

Two buttons, twenty-five seconds, no homework. The whole idea fits between the huddle breaking and the snap.

STEP 1

The moment pops up

A big call lands — 4th & short, a two-point try, an onside kick. Your phone buzzes with just the situation and two big buttons. Nothing else to read.

STEP 2

Everybody votes at once

One tap before the snap. The crowd split appears the second the play starts — so nobody can vote with hindsight and pretend they knew all along.

STEP 3

The result grades everyone

Your call, the crowd's call, the coach's call, and what the numbers said — all four on the record, all four graded by what happened. Season standings keep the receipts.
