The workings, underneath

Seventeen games stand here — more than any other shelf — and several are trainers in game dress: Keep It keeps what you learn, Look Closer and Sharp Eyes train the eye, and The Quiet Room keeps your guessing results honest. And the kit at the top of the page runs this shelf as one: the filter button with this kind’s name shows it alone, the games search filters it as you type — reading every game’s name and description — and “A–Z” lifts every game onto one flat list while “By category” sends them home.
