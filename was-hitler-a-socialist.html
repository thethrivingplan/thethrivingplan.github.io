Was Hitler a socialist?

FALSE

Historians are not split on this. The claim fails on the record, in four simple facts.

The claim

“Hitler was a far left socialist. Literally in the name of his party!”

Posted by Elon Musk on X, 23 October 2026 — at least his third time making this claim this year. (indy100)

First, the word

Socialism means workers and the public owning the means of production, and equality over hierarchy. Keep that definition in hand — Hitler fails every part of it.

Why it’s false — four facts

  1. The Holocaust Museum calls the Nazi Party far-right

    The US Holocaust Memorial Museum’s Holocaust Encyclopedia describes the Nazi Party as a “far-right racist and antisemitic political party” that campaigned to eradicate “Marxism” — the Nazis’ own word for both communism and socialism. Source: indy100, quoting the Holocaust Encyclopedia

  2. In power, he banned the socialists

    In 1933 Hitler banned the Social Democratic and Communist parties and outlawed trade unions. Dachau, the first concentration camp, was built for left-wing opponents. Source: Full Fact

  3. He murdered his own party’s left wing

    The Strasser brothers led the Nazi faction that actually wanted socialist policies. Otto broke away in 1930; Gregor was killed in the Night of the Long Knives in 1934. Source: Full Fact

  4. He protected private property — and the “socialist” in the name was a slogan

    Hitler courted Germany’s big industrialists, took their funding, defended private property, and privatized state-owned industry. The word “socialist” was added to the party’s name in 1920 to attract working-class voters — a recruiting label, not a program. Sources: Full Fact · OurHistory

The name proves nothing

A name is a label, not evidence. North Korea calls itself the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea. Nobody checks the label — you check what the regime actually did.

“Hitler was never a socialist.”

— Ian Kershaw, historian and leading Hitler biographer, quoted by Full Fact
