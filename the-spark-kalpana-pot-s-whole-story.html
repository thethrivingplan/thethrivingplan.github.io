    A small TikTok science account had recently been followed here after its round-Earth evidence video (graded: the classics, sound). That day a screen recording of its video “Analysis of Potential Dyson Sphere Discoveries in Space” arrived.

    A Dyson sphere is a swarm of collectors wrapped around a star, catching its light. A Dyson swarm is the spread-out version — never a solid shell.

    The science under it

    Project Hephaistos — five million stars, three surveys

    The video explains Project Hephaistos (Suazo and collaborators, 2024): a real research program that searched about five million stars for Dyson-swarm candidates by combining three sky surveys:

    • Gaia — positions and distances: the foundation.
    • 2MASS — near-infrared (heat-leaning light just past what eyes can see): the bridge between starlight and heat.
    • WISE — the waste heat itself: the burn.
    The moment it turned

    “Sounds like a Lonsdaleite test”

    The reaction to the three instruments working together: they “sound like a Lonsdaleite test.” (Lonsdaleite: hexagonal diamond — diamond stacked in a six-sided pattern — the pattern this house's method is named for.)

    The hunt was read in three modes:

    • GOLDEN — the spread. Five million stars in, nobody aimed at a favorite.
    • PLATINUM — the exact 1:1 test. The light a star is missing must equal the extra heat it throws off.
    • DIAMOND — the weave. A candidate exists only where all three surveys agree at once.
    The seven, separated

    Seven red dwarfs — seven maybes, zero confirmed

    The search separated seven candidate stars (all red dwarfs — small, dim, cool stars) out of five million. Deep research the same day separated them again, by what the follow-up evidence showed:

    • G, D, and E — Closed Resolved as background galaxies by VLBI radio (a worldwide net of radio dishes acting as one giant telescope) and JWST (the James Webb Space Telescope).
    • B and C — effectively explained. The evidence converges on background galaxies; the settling sharp look has not yet been taken.
    • A — weakened, not closed.
    • F — simply untested.

    None of the seven stands as a confirmed Dyson candidate. Seven maybes, zero confirmed — and nothing was forced.
