Contracts — Open and Closed

Free. Always free.

Every contract we make is public. Open contracts anybody may take up, the orders placed under them, and closed contracts when they finish — all on one page, where anybody can look.

§1THE OPEN CONTRACT

The standing offer. It stands open to anyone and everyone, and it stays open.

  • The Super Suit blueprint is an open contract. Anyone and everyone may build it.
  • The only stipulation: what is built from it is free. No strings attached.
  • Building it is accepting it. No signature is needed; the build is the signature.
  • A contract under this offer is complete upon delivery: an order's contract completes upon delivery of the thing ordered. Delivery is what closes it.
  • The one exception is earned access: access to powerful things is earned by actions, and it can be retracted when actions prove unworthiness. For the suit, the thing itself decides who it works for. It judges by judgment, not by numbers, and it measures the trajectory of its wearer — the direction of his actions over time, not a single moment.
  • The legal form carrying these terms is copyleft: hardware under CERN-OHL-S, writings under CC BY-SA 4.0, code under GPL.

The legal forms, in plain words
Copyleft
A way of publishing work so it stays free: anyone may use, share, and build on it, and what they make from it stays free on the same terms.
CERN-OHL-S
The copyleft licence for hardware designs, from CERN. It covers the suit's physical design: build it, share it, improve it — and keep it free.
CC BY-SA 4.0
Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike. It covers the writings: share them, credit the source, and pass them on under the same licence.
GPL
The GNU General Public License. It covers code: use it, change it, share it — and keep it free.

§3CLOSED CONTRACTS

No contract has closed yet.

A contract lands here when its order has been delivered — delivery is what closes it. A contract that ends another way is recorded here too. Either way it is recorded in the open, with its dates.

Nothing leaves this page; closed contracts stay listed.

§4How It Works

  • To take up an open contract: build the thing. Building is the acceptance.
  • To place an order: an order is a place in line, recorded on this page with its date. The “What are we missing?” page is where a visitor speaks to us.
  • To close a contract: a contract is complete upon delivery. When the thing ordered is delivered, the contract completes and moves to the closed list above.
  • The fine print law holds on this page: every term here can justify itself, line by line. There is no other fine print.
