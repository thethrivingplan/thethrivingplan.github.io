About this hub ▾

Health is one of the seven hubs on the start page, with a web of its own — built the same way as the start: the search sits at the center, the doors hang around it, and every tap leaves for a page of its own instead of opening inside the web.

These pages teach and state facts — the simulators show how care works from the inside, the eye tests are games you play, and the plain-facts page states what a thing is. None of it diagnoses or treats anybody. For your own health, a real clinician who can see you is the door that matters.
