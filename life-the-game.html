The point, printed plain

Money is out of the equation entirely. Points in this game can never be bought, sold, traded, or transferred. There is nothing to buy and nobody selling. A point only ever means one thing: “I did the thing.”

This game exists so people spend time working on themselves and growing — not to make anybody better at making somebody else money. Your effort pays you, in rewards you chose, at prices you set.

Why it works: when the reward can't be purchased, the only way to earn it is to actually live the quest. The shortcut doesn't exist, so the growth is the game.

Go deeper: most games sell you the feeling of progress. Here the feeling and the fact are the same thing — the points on this screen rose because something real happened in your actual day. That's the whole design, and it's why prices and point values are yours to set: you know what a day off is worth to you.

The honor system — the honest core

This game cannot check whether you really did the push-ups. Nothing here watches you, counts your reps, or verifies a thing. It runs on the honor system, completely.

Your record is your own mirror. Tapping “complete” on a quest you didn't do doesn't fool a server somewhere — it just fogs the mirror you're looking into. Cheating here only cheats the person in it.

And gently: there are no streaks to lose, no punishments, no points taken away for a hard week. A missed day is just a day. The game will be here when you are.

Why no checking? because the person this game serves is you. A mirror doesn't need a guard — it needs you to look at it honestly.

Go deeper: the four stats are a balance check, not a contest. If Strength climbs while Heart sits at zero for a month, the mirror is telling you something about where your days are going — no judgment attached, just information you can act on.

Stock a new reward

Price it so it feels earned, not free and not impossible.

Why you set the prices: a candy bar and a day off are not worth the same in your life, and only you know the exchange rate between your effort and your treats.

Go deeper: if a reward sits unclaimed for weeks, its price may be too high or the reward may not actually tempt you. Adjust either one. A shop tuned to what you truly want pulls you through quests on the days motivation is thin — that's not weakness, that's design.

    Your data — yours, on your device

    This game keeps everything on this device, in this page — nothing is sent anywhere, there's no account and no server holding your record. Your game lives in this tab: export it to a file to keep it, and import that file any time to pick up where you left off.

    Export saves your points, stats, quests, rewards, and mirror as one small file on your device. Forget erases it all from here, for good — export first if any of it matters to you.

    Forget everything? Your points, stats, quests, rewards, and mirror will be erased and the game restarts fresh. This cannot be undone.
