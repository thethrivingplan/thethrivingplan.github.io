The Freckle Map Game

Eight freckles, five ways to read them. Tap a reading to switch it in. Everything here is a game — loose fits and near misses welcome — except the last one, which is the real thing, for contrast.

The board above is an AI-generated example illustration — not a real photo.

How to read the board: Treasure Map and Songline fit the grammar of maps (a place, a path, a destination) better than they fit any specific real map. Runes is the loosest — shapes stretched toward meanings. Dipper Squint shows the cheat openly: one freckle has to be ignored. Blaschko is not a game reading at all — those sweeping curves are the actual hidden routes skin cells take, and the freckles don't follow them. Fun on top, facts on the bottom — labels on everything.
