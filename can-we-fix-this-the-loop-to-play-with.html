How to play — Tap to open
  1. On the visitor's page below, pick a fix and send it — try the crooked sign first.
  2. Watch it appear in the Inbox (Part 2), marked Waiting.
  3. Go to Muse's chair (Part 3) and tap Fix it. The fix is published — but the visitor's page does not change yet.
  4. Back on the visitor's page, tap the big Refresh button. That's the moment the fix arrives.

Then try the other doors: new headline words, a new banner color, your own words, even a fix somebody just said out loud.
