1  The Venn — corruption ∩ behaviour

Two circles, not one bucket. Corruption is money, office and public trust. Inappropriate behaviour is conduct. Some pieces honestly sit in both — the lens in the middle — and they are labelled as overlap, never used to blur the rest.

How to read it: a piece's centre decides its circle. The 2024 case sits in the lens because — as filed — it is about business records and concealment of behaviour. That does not move the other four.

Corruption only

Trump University — $25M settlement (2016)

Settled Nov 2016, expressly no admission of wrongdoing. Closed.

Corruption only

Trump Foundation — dissolved, $2M to charities

Court-supervised dissolution. Per the AG, Trump admitted personally misusing funds; board had not met since 1999. Paid & closed Dec 2019. No lifetime ban imposed.

Corruption only

Trump Organization tax fraud — 17 counts

Jury convicted Trump Corp. & Trump Payroll Corp. on all 17 counts (Dec 2022); $1.61M fine. Trump was not a defendant — printed in the record. Companies' appeal outcome: filed as UNVERIFIED.

Corruption only

NY civil fraud — liability stands, money struck

Fraud liability finding stands, but the $354.9M disgorgement (~$454M with interest) was vacated in full on 21 Aug 2025 as an excessive fine. Now at the NY Court of Appeals — no decision as of 27 Oct 2026.

Overlap — in both

2024 criminal case — 34 counts

Conviction on all 34 counts stands (unconditional discharge, Jan 2025). Direct appeal pending in the First Department; federal-removal track denied a third time 28 Aug 2026. Filed in the lens: money/records used to conceal behaviour.

Behaviour side

Epstein Files — 17 records, kept separate

Includes Giuffre's sworn testimony (“never saw Trump participate”), Chief Reiter's 2019 FBI 302, Maxwell's 2025 DOJ interview (filed with its weight: unsworn, convicted, interested source), the black-book entry, and the 1992 NBC footage. Both directions filed, contradictions left standing.

4  Ties — how strong is the thread?

Every tie in Batch 01 (8 records) carries its grade on its face. A thread that ends is a finding, not a failure.

1Direct dealing

Sater — strongest on paper: 1998 racketeering plea, Bayrock / Trump SoHo, 2010 “Senior Advisor” card. Weichselbaum — most personal: helicopter vendor, apartment on cash-plus-services, character letter; served 18 months.

2Documented association

S&A Concrete — Salerno / Castellano ownership; 1986 indictment citing ~$7.8M Trump Plaza subcontract. LiButti — two adjudicated casino fines; Gotti / Gambino association per the regulatory record.

3Shared intermediary

Roy Cohn — filed as the intermediary he was, not upgraded to a direct tie.

4Allegation only

Barrett-claimed 1983 Trump–Salerno meeting: single-source, filed as Grade 4 — not as fact. FinCEN Taj Mahal fines ($477,700 in 1998; $10M in 2015 after control surrendered) filed as laundering vulnerability, not a mob dealing.

5Searched — no evidence

Secret societies: Freemasonry, Skull & Bones, Bohemian Grove, Knights of Malta, Bilderberg, CFR — no documented membership found. Insider evidence points the other way. Fred Trump 1927: arrest documented, charge dismissed, Klan membership not proven.
