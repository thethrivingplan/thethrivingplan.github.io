How it works

Two doors, same board. No account wall to look — you only share contact details when you post or apply.

🔨 Hiring someone

  1. Post the job — what, where, when, and what it pays. Plain numbers beat “competitive pay.”
  2. Compare pros — ratings, jobs done, and rates side by side, Angie's List style.
  3. Reach out your way — the Hire button on any pro card builds a ready-to-send message: copy or download it, send it from your own email or messages, and agree price and scope in writing before work starts.

🧰 Finding work

  1. Apply in one tap — the Apply button builds your application note; copy it or send it your way.
  2. Or list yourself — add a pro card below so hirers come to you.
  3. Get rated fairly — every finished job can add a ★ rating to your card.
