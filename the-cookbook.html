    About this cookbook

    What it is: a starter collection of classic, documented dishes — pancakes, chili, a sandwich loaf, béchamel — each written from scratch in our own words. Ingredient lists are facts; the instructions, notes, and swaps here are original writing, and no other cookbook’s text or photos were copied.

    What it means: where two honest ways exist — butter or oil, fresh or dried herbs — both are printed with their trade-offs, and the choice stays yours. Where a recipe does not scale cleanly, the scaler says so instead of pretending the math is the whole story.

    What it could mean for you: cook one recipe as written, then cook it again your way, and save your version in Your Recipes above. That is how a cookbook becomes yours.

    Why → How → Act: Why — good cooking knowledge should be free and plainly written. How — original recipes, teaching in the step where it matters, and videos only where seeing it genuinely helps. Act — pick a recipe, set your servings, and cook.
