Make your own fan page

Fan groups live scattered all over the internet, on other people's platforms, under other people's rules. This is the other way: your own page, about your own thing, that you own outright.

Disc golf. Blackjack and cards. Pottery. LARPing (live action role-playing). A band, a book series, a bakers' obsession — if you're a fan of it, it fits here.

Adult topics are allowed too — adult books, adult gatherings, whatever your thing honestly is. If it's for grown-ups, you set the Adults only switch below and your page opens with a plain 18+ gate. The gate is yours to set; nobody here decides your topic for you.

Take your page with you

When it reads the way you want it, download it. You get one single file — your whole fan page in one piece — made right here in your browser.

Plainly: nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere when you download — the file is built on your device, from your words. Your page is yours. It does not join our catalog and it is never hosted by us. Share it wherever you like: send the file to friends, put it on any free web host, post the text version anywhere text goes.

Your page carries one small line at the bottom — “Made with the free fan page template — free, always free” — with a door back to this builder, so the next fan can make theirs. The template is copyleft (CC BY-SA 4.0 — free to share and remix, credit kept, same freedom passed on).
