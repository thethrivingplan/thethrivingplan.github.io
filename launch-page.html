Launch Page

One place to release a video everywhere it can go. You pick what gets released. You say “release it.” The full fan-out runs, and you get one receipt at the end — every live link, plus anything parked and why.

Working name — “Launch Page” is easy to change when a better name lands. Releases fire on your word, when a video is ready. The timing slots further down are paper recommendations only, never a schedule you have to follow.

The Treatment Gate

No selection = no treatment. Nothing runs on its own.

The treatment — filing, the Quality Gate, optimization, and the fan-out — runs only on what Tristan selects. An inventory, a sweep, or a documentation pass never selects anything by itself; it only hands him the list to pick from.

A selection is one of three shapes:

  1. One specific post he names.
  2. A start date he names — everything from that date forward is selected.
  3. A bunch he picks as a group, instead of one by one.

The release flow

Surface simple, depth one tap down. Tap a door to open it — nothing is removed, just layered.

2Quality Gate
PASS required before anything posts — the gate outranks speed▾
  • Audio level, measured numerically — never ship a silent or whisper-quiet edit.
  • Sync check — audio↔picture offset is measured; beyond ~40 ms fails the gate until corrected.
  • Honest labels — AI-created music is tagged as AI-created, at the true level (AI-generated vs AI-assisted). An untagged AI track fails the gate like any other labeling failure.
  • People tags — nobody is tagged without their standing yes. One ask, reversible.
  • Tags & hashtags — accurate and targeted, never spammy or misleading.
  • Nothing posts under Tristan's name that Tristan didn't make.

A failed gate stops the blast. Full stop.

3Optimize — without sacrificing
Audio ≈ −14 LUFS · true peak ≈ −1 dB · picture never re-encoded▾
  • Audio loudness to ≈ −14 LUFS, true peak ≈ −1 dB (linear loudnorm).
  • The video stream is copied bit-for-bit, never re-encoded; faststart on.
  • Sync correction rides this same step: only the audio stream shifts, by the measured offset (Bluetooth chains typically run ~150–300 ms late). The picture is never touched.
  • If the offset drifts mid-recording, it's corrected per segment — and the receipt says so.
  • The corrected file is re-measured; residual target < ~20 ms. Before/after numbers ride the receipt.
4Cover frame
A still for the platforms that require one▾

An extracted still travels with the packet wherever a platform needs a cover — so the Reel, Story, and Short all land looking intentional, not random.

5Fan-out
Same approved packet everywhere: title / description / honest tags▾

The doors for each platform are in the next section, in posting order. One approval covers the whole fan-out — no per-platform re-asking beyond the approval cards the services themselves require.

6One receipt
Every live link, plus anything parked or failed — and why▾

The release ends with a single receipt, not six separate reports. It always flags the two personal Facebook phone steps (Reel + Story) as Tristan's remaining steps — and credits him if he's already done them, which he has beaten the reminder on before.

Both personal steps retire the day the connector door opens and the release becomes one button end-to-end.

“Release it.”

That's the whole trigger. One approval → the full fan-out above → one receipt. Timing recommendations never hold a ready video hostage.

How the doors are built — four points

Tristan's design calls, stated plainly so anyone reading this page knows what to expect.

1

Consent doors thread straight through

Where a platform asks permission, the door goes straight to its consent screen — one tap lands on the consent screen, one Allow, and the door stays open. No maze, no re-asking tour.

2

Some doors don't exist — yet

The personal Facebook Reel and Story have no permission door to give. They stay Tristan's ~1-minute phone steps until the connector gains the ability — labeled as his steps, never as failures.

3

YouTube Shorts ride the same click

Shorts fold into the single release click — one click and done, no separate verify. The only named exception is a giant render that hits the file-size wall.

4

Sign-ins are once-ever

Not zero-ever — once-ever. Each platform needs one successful sign-in; after that, the door stays open. TikTok is the one still waiting on its first, parked at the QR scan.

Underneath

The deeper layers — here when you want them, out of the way when you don't.

◷Timing — recommendation only
Releases fire on Tristan's word, not on a slot▾

Every packet carries a timing recommendation, drawn from 2026 studies — and that's all it is. In practice, releases fire when Tristan says so.

  • Primary slot: Thursday 6:00 PM CT — the overlap of the Shorts and Reels data (Buffer: 1.8M Shorts, 9.6M Reels; SocialPilot; Metricool).
  • Alternate: Friday 4:30 PM CT — when a video is YouTube-first.
  • Honest scale: vidIQ (40M videos) puts the day-of-week effect under ~6% by day 30. Timing is a small multiplier; consistency is the big lever. Study averages are never presented as Tristan's own analytics.
  • As his channel data grows, his own audience hours (YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience) override the study slots, and the receipt notes the change.
↗Walls & the standing expansion order
“Post it anywhere and everywhere possible, as soon as their walls come down.”▾

The moment a platform becomes reachable — login solved, account created, connector appears — it joins the standing fan-out automatically, and releases still in their posting window go up there too, under the original approval.

Doors that could widen the blast (Tristan creates the account; posting follows once it's connected): X (Twitter) — short video · Snapchat — Spotlight · Reddit — video subs · Pinterest — video pins · Bluesky — video posts.

No capability is claimed before it exists. A door that can't exist yet is labeled honestly as a phone step or a parked door — see TikTok and the personal Facebook steps above.

§Rules that never bend
The short list the whole flow answers to▾
  • The Quality Gate outranks speed. A failed gate stops the blast.
  • The Treatment Gate outranks everything: no selection, no treatment.
  • AI-created music is always tagged as AI-created, at the true level — honestly, not as a bad thing.
  • No tagging people without their standing yes.
  • Tags and hashtags: accurate and targeted, never spammy or misleading.
  • Nothing posts under Tristan's name that Tristan didn't make.
  • One approval, full fan-out, one receipt.
