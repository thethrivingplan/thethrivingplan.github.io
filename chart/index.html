The aspects — how the planets talk

Aspects are angles between planets — conversations between drives. Flowing ones (trine, sextile) run easily; tense ones (square, opposition) create friction that produces energy; a conjunction fuses the two into one. The orb is how close to exact the angle is — smaller means louder. Each line below says what the conversation is about.

    New to this? What you're looking at
    Sun sign — the core self: what you're here to become, where your vitality lives.
    Moon sign — the inner weather: feelings, instincts, what makes you feel safe.
    Rising sign (Ascendant) — the front door: the style the world meets first. Needs a birth time.
    Planets in signs — each planet is a drive (Mercury thinks, Venus loves, Mars pushes…); the sign is how it does its job.
    Houses — the twelve life areas (work, home, partnership…). A planet's house says where its energy plays out. Needs a birth time.
    Aspects — the angles between planets: their conversations. Trines (120°) and sextiles (60°) flow easily; squares (90°) and oppositions (180°) argue and generate energy; conjunctions (0°) fuse two drives into one.
    Orb — how close an aspect is to exact. Smaller orb = louder conversation.
    Retrograde (℞) — a planet only appearing to move backwards. Nothing actually reverses: the planets orbit at different speeds, so from Earth, when a faster orbit catches and passes a slower one, the other planet seems to loop backwards against the stars — the way a car you overtake seems to roll backwards against the landscape. The tradition reads that planet's drive as turned inward: reviewed, rehearsed, done in its own way. Every retrograde planet in a cast chart gets its own plain-English card in the reading. It's also the source of the famous saying — “Mercury's in retrograde” as the explanation for a glitchy week — which the “Right now in the stars” section takes apart plainly: what people are claiming, and the honest check beside it.
    Elements — Fire (spark), Earth (ground), Air (ideas), Water (feeling). A missing element speaks as loudly as a full one.
    Chart ruler — the planet ruling your rising sign; it steers the whole chart.
    Sign-to-sign touch — how any two signs sit on the wheel to each other (same, flowing, clashing, opposite…). The angle is exact geometry; what it means is the tradition's interpretation — in the “How it all touches” layer of the reading, labelled as such.
