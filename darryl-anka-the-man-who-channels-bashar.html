Darryl Anka — the man who channels Bashar

A Hollywood visual-effects craftsman who has spent more than forty years saying an extraterrestrial speaks through him — and who tells his own audience, on the record, that they don't have to believe it. This summary keeps the layers separate: the documented man, the claimed being, the teachings on their own merits, and the record as it checks out.

1 · The man

Fact Darryl Anka was born on October 12, 1951, in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, and grew up in Southern California. He is a first cousin of singer Paul Anka — confirmed by interviewer Mayim Bialik in her own writing. Sources: Memory Alpha; Bialik Breakdown (Substack).

The film career

Fact Before Bashar there was a workbench. From the late 1970s Anka worked in Hollywood visual effects — miniatures, models, props, storyboards, set and design work — largely in uncredited craft roles. Documented credits include uncredited model maker on Star Trek: The Motion Picture (1979), uncredited prop maker on Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan (1982), and miniature crew on Star Trek Nemesis (2002), where his name appears in the ACM SIGGRAPH history archive's crew listing. His standard biography additionally lists Iron Man, The Aviator, Pirates of the Caribbean: At World's End, I, Robot, Live Free or Die Hard, and Flags of Our Fathers. Sources: Memory Alpha; SIGGRAPH history archive; RationalWiki.

His own films and books

Fact Through Zia Films LLC — the production company he owns with his wife and co-producer Erica Jordan — Anka has written, directed, and produced three films: Dearly Departed (2013, a fictional documentary about the afterlife), First Contact (a documentary about his own channeling, narrated by James Woods), and Alienated (a sci-fi romance). He also appeared as himself in the 2008 channeling documentary Tuning In, alongside Lee Carroll (Kryon) and others. Contested First Contact's year: release records give a premiere/release of September 16, 2016 (runtime 1h 35m), while some filmographies list it as 2015 — likely the production year.

Fact His books span nearly four decades: The New Metaphysics (1987); Bashar: Blueprint for Change (1990, compiled from session transcripts of 1986–89); Contact Cards (1996); Quest for Truth: 100 Insights That Could Change Your Life (1997, with Steve Meyers); the sci-fi novels Shards of a Shattered Mirror Book I: Cryptic (2017) and Book II: Nocturnal (2020); The Masters of Limitation (2020); and The Formula: The Instruction Manual for Living Your Best Life (October 31, 2024). His bios commonly claim “over 20 books published in the U.S. and Japan.” Contested Japan sales: secondary sources claim either ~300,000 books sold there or “half a million copies in Japan alone” — the figures conflict and neither is verified.

Fact Bios also state that he runs a Los Angeles escape-room business — spelled “Boggled” in one bio and “Fogled” in a podcast transcript, so the spelling itself is uncertain.

The operation today

Fact Bashar Communications, Inc. is a California corporation run by Anka with business partner April Rochelle (CEO); its own About page signs its mission statement “Darryl Anka and April Rochelle.” Its published mailing address is 22647 Ventura Blvd #277, Woodland Hills, CA 91364, and Anka himself lives in Woodland Hills. As of November 2026 the work runs as: monthly live-stream channeling events on the BasharTV platform; in-person events (Sedona, Palm Springs, and San Diego retreats; Conscious Life Expo; The Channel Panel); a large paid recordings catalog; private Zoom sessions (booking currently paused for 2026); a merchandise store; and a multi-year “Interstellar Alliance Social Experiment,” rolled out from 2024, whose handouts are linked free from the homepage. Sources: bashar.org — Bashar Communications; bashar.org homepage (both read live, Nov 10, 2026).

2 · How the channeling began

Everything in this section is Claim — Anka's own account, from his official biography page. The events are not independently verifiable, and they're told here the way he tells them. Source: bashar.org — Darryl Anka biography (read live, Nov 10, 2026); corroborating detail from his June 14, 2014 Buddha at the Gas Pump interview.

1973: two sightings

“On two occasions within the same week in 1973, I had close-range, broad-daylight sightings of UFOs with witnesses present both times.”— Darryl Anka, official biography

He describes a dark metallic triangular craft, about 30 feet on each side, with three blue-white lights and one orange-red center light — roughly 150 feet away the first time. The second distance is given as about 60 feet on his official page and about 70 feet in his 2014 interview; the witnesses were family and close friends. The sightings set off years of reading on UFOs and metaphysics.

1983: the meditation class

Ten years later, after listening to another channel for several months, Anka joined that channel's class — in his words, “not intending to become a channel myself.” Midway through, during guided meditations, he says he received a telepathic message and instantly “knew” three things: the consciousness was the extraterrestrial connected to the 1973 sightings — its “ship”; he had made a pre-birth agreement to channel this being; and the time had come, if he still chose to proceed.

His own hedge, from the same page, is worth printing in full — it's the sentence the whole forty-year record hangs on:

“…even if it wasn't really another entity, even if it was some mysterious portion of my own consciousness, the information… could be used to help people… Whatever the source, I decided to continue.”— Darryl Anka, official biography

Per the 2014 interview, the early validation that mattered to him included the class teacher's entity and a classmate producing a sketch of Bashar; he then channeled for a doctoral researcher studying channeling, with audiences growing from about 5 to 20 to 40 people, twice weekly, and eventually to auditoriums.

Contested The start year: Anka's biography says he has channeled publicly since 1983; the official “Who Is Bashar” page says Bashar “has spoken since 1984.” Best statement: onset in 1983, public channeling from 1983–84. A December 3, 1986 Los Angeles Times account already reported him channeling since 1983, so the practice is documented from near its start either way.

3 · The being, as claimed

Everything in this section is Claim — how Anka and Bashar Communications present Bashar. None of it is independently verifiable.

“Bashar is a physical E.T, a friend from the future who has spoken since 1984 through channel Darryl Anka.”— bashar.org — Who Is Bashar (read live, Nov 10, 2026)
  • Role: “First Contact Specialist” for his civilization — the being whose job is introducing his people to other worlds.
  • Home: Essassani (“place of living light”); his people are the Sassani. It is placed about 500 light-years away in the direction of Orion — but in a parallel reality, so it is not locatable in our universe.
  • Form: a human–Grey hybrid. Anka described him in a March 2026 interview as about 5 feet tall, slender, white-skinned, with larger eyes, smaller ears, nose and mouth, and no hair.
  • Future self: Bashar presents himself as a future version of Anka himself. Asked directly — “Bashar is some future version of yourself?” — Anka answered: “Yes, that's the way he presents himself.”
  • The name: per Anka's biography, “Bashar” is an Arabic word meaning “messenger,” more specifically “bringer of good news,” chosen because Anka's heritage is half-Arabic (he does not speak Arabic). It is not Bashar's literal name — the telepathic Essassani, in the account, do not use names.

Contested How far in the future? The long-standing figure in most summaries is about 300 years. But about 700 years appears in material about Willa, another being Anka channels, and some 2026 coverage describes Essassani as about 3,000 years more advanced than Earth. The sources do not agree, and no single official figure could be confirmed on bashar.org's own pages.

Anka's own framing of what channeling is

Fact — these are his documented positions, whatever one makes of the channeling itself:

  • A natural, universal state. Anka defines channeling as a gamma brainwave state (“between 40 and 100 cycles per second”) that everyone enters when absorbed in something they love — being “in the zone” and losing track of time. “Everyone actually channels from time to time… we wanted to demystify the concept of channeling.” His film First Contact includes EEG recordings of his brain while channeling, presented as demystification — his presentation, not peer-reviewed science.
  • A blend, not a pure transmission. What comes through, he says, is a blend or “simulacrum” — “not getting the pure personality of the channel, and… not the pure personality of the entity either.” During sessions he says he largely does not hear or remember the words, likening it to being lost in a daydream.
  • Belief explicitly not required. From his official biography:
“I always make it clear to people that they do not have to believe that Bashar is really an extraterrestrial… If they want to believe the words are coming from another part of my own consciousness, that's fine with me. I have no way of proving Bashar's existence to anyone anyway.”— Darryl Anka, official biography
  • The “permission slip.” Inside the teachings themselves, Bashar — like crystals, rituals, and techniques — is framed as a permission slip: a symbol people use to give themselves permission to be more of who they are. That is Anka's and Bashar's own deflationary framing of what the channeling is for, and it matters for reading everything in the next section.

4 · The teachings

All of this section is Claim — Anka/Bashar doctrine. The wording quoted is documented from the sources cited; whether the ideas are true is a separate question from where they supposedly come from, and each reader grades that part against their own life.

The Five Laws

Exact official wording, from bashar.org/fivelaws (read live, Nov 10, 2026):

  1. You exist.
  2. Everything is here and now.
  3. The One is the All and the All are the One.
  4. What you put out is what you get back.
  5. Everything changes except the first 4 Laws.

Basic principles

From the official principles page (bashar.org/principles, read live):

  • “You are a non-physical consciousness that is experiencing physical reality.”
  • “The highest purpose of your life is to be yourself to the best of your ability and live each moment as fully as possible.”
  • “You always have free will and the freedom to choose.”
  • “You are naturally abundant, and your choices are always supported by creation.”
  • “You create the past and the future from the here and now.”
  • “Excitement is the physical translation of the vibrational resonance that is your true, core natural [being].”

The Formula — follow your highest excitement

The teaching's operating instructions, from bashar.org/formula (read live):

  1. “Act on your excitement, your passion, whatever is most exciting to you, in the moment. Do this every moment that you can.”
  2. “Do this to the best of your ability. Take it as far as you can go until you cannot take it any further.”
  3. “Act on your excitement/passion with absolutely no insistence, assumption or expectation of what the outcome should be.”
  4. “Choose to remain in a positive state regardless of what happens.”
  5. “Constantly investigate your belief systems. Release & replace the un-preferred beliefs: fear-based beliefs…”

In one line: “Act on your excitement, to the best of your ability, with absolutely no insistence, assumption or expectation of what the outcome should be.” In the teaching, excitement is not mere fun — it is the physical translation of the vibration of one's true core being, a compass signal from the “higher mind.” Following the Formula is said to switch on synchronicity: the right things arriving in the right order because you're finally walking your own path.

Abundance and money

From Quest for Truth (1997), the Bashar text, full text hosted on archive.org:

“Abundance is the ability to do what you need to do, when you need to do it. Period.”— Bashar, in Quest for Truth (1997)

On money specifically: “Now yes, on your planet we understand you have one symbol of abundance called money. Yes, it's valid; yes, it's creative… It is just as creative as any other way to represent abundance.” Insisting abundance arrive only as money, the teaching says, makes people “abundance-blind” to its other forms — gifts, synchronicity, imagination, exchange and trade, communication (elsewhere systematized as the Five Forms of Abundance, of which money is only one). The practical instruction: “Relax the need and the insistence of abundance coming a particular way.”

The same corpus carries the permission-slip doctrine in full: “All tools, all techniques, all knowledge is just permission slips to allow yourself to give yourself permission to be more of who you are” — and money is explicitly treated as one such symbol. And the book opens with a line consistent with everything in section 3: “Do not believe anything I say just because I said it.”

Fact Why, then, aren't the sessions free? Bashar's on-record answer (October 2, 2026, “The Open Contact Progress Report”): the knowledge itself is free and accessible to anyone who develops the ability; attendees pay for the service of delivery — his analogy is buying food versus growing it yourself. Section 5 prices that service at full size.

Parallel realities — “everything is here and now”

In the teaching, reality is a sequence of static frames; consciousness shifts through “billions of parallel realities every second,” creating the illusion of motion and time. You never change a world — you change yourself, and thereby shift to a parallel Earth that already matches the change. All versions of reality already exist and coexist “here and now” (Law 2); the past and future are perspectives from the present. The long-standing unofficial summary's analogy: a radio does not create the program it hears — it tunes to a simultaneously existing broadcast, and beliefs are the tuner.

What's free

Fact The core teachings cost nothing: the Five Laws, the Formula, and the Basic Principles are posted free as guides on bashar.org; the Interstellar Alliance Social Experiment handouts are free from the homepage; a free replay period follows each live stream before the recording goes on sale; extensive highlight clips run free on the official YouTube and social channels; and the film First Contact streams free on ad-supported services (Tubi/Fawesome, per Moviefone).

Giving, lawsuits, complaints — the negative findings

Fact Charitable giving: none documented. Searches on November 10, 2026 found no donations, foundation, or charity partnerships by Anka or Bashar Communications. (The flow runs the other way on paper: BasharTV carries a “Donate” link and bashar.org a “Support the Bashar Library” item — the organization solicits donations; that is not giving by it. Private giving, if any exists, would not necessarily be public.)

Fact Lawsuits, fraud findings, formal complaints: none found. No lawsuits against Anka or Bashar Communications, no court or regulator fraud findings, no formal complaints — and no BBB business profile or BBB complaints for Bashar Communications. This is a web-search-level negative, not a court-records search. Related items that are not legal actions against Anka: Channel 5 host Andrew Callaghan says the organization gave him a lifetime ban from Bashar events after covert filming, and his crew was ejected from the October 2026 Sedona event; one attendee there threatened a lawsuit against the film crew, not against Anka.

One figure to handle carefully: a skeptical critic speculates Anka “has amassed a fortune (of somewhere around $15 million or more?)” — that is a critic's guess, printed here as speculation, not a documented net worth.

What the skeptics say

Fact — that these criticisms are made: RationalWiki's entry labels him “a spiritual medium and kook.” Project Worldview's critical essay is titled “Bashar: UFO religious celebrity and fraud?” Channel 5's October 2026 pieces (“Bashar's Alien Cult”; “THE US ALIEN CHANNELER ECONOMY”) treat the operation satirically, as a paid “alien channeler economy.” The recurring criticisms across sources: no empirical evidence that Bashar is an extraterrestrial (even sympathetic explainers concede this); charging money for messages said to be intended for humanity's benefit; prediction dates that shift over time; and the possibility that the persona is performance or acting. Anka/Bashar's own counter-framing distinguishes welcomed “skepticism” (open-minded inquiry) from “cynicism” (closed rejection).

The 2027 claim — dated, labeled, and testable

Claim Contact timelines in the material have moved over time. In May 2012, Bashar gave an open-contact window of 2025–2033, with the Yahyel (a hybrid civilization) as first contactors. The current framing, in 2025–26 sessions, calls 2026 “the Year of Disclosure” and targets 2027 (into 2028) for extraterrestrials to be introduced through official channels.

Two things can be said plainly. First, inside Bashar's own framework these are never fixed prophecies: timelines are probabilities read off the current energy, and they shift when people shift — one BasharTV Short is literally titled “Will Contact in 2027 Be 100% Certain?” Second, as of this page's research date (November 10, 2026) the window has not yet arrived and is not checkable. It grades itself: if alien life is blatantly obvious through official channels by the end of 2027, the claim upgrades; if not, that files itself too.

6 · Influence

Fact The Formula circulates far beyond Bashar's own channels. TikTok and Instagram manifestation teachers re-teach the five steps, usually crediting Bashar; explainer and quote sites reproduce the Formula and the abundance definition verbatim — Next Level Soul's multi-part “complete guide,” Conscious Life News, The Keys to Joy — and teaching workbooks from unrelated groups quote them too. The sentence “abundance is the ability to do what you need to do when you need to do it” now travels mostly detached from any spaceship.

Fact Mainstream platforms keep booking him. Mayim Bialik's Bialik Breakdown featured Anka in March 2026 episodes; Bialik wrote that she “loved talking with Darryl” and promoted his “2026 is the year of alien contact” framing. Danica Patrick's Pretty Intense podcast has hosted him. Alex Ferrari's Next Level Soul has run multiple long interviews and carries First Contact on NLS TV. Gaia streams First Contact and a “Messages from Bashar” series. Back in 2008, Tuning In placed him alongside the era's most prominent channels.

Fact Academics noticed. Francesco Baroni's CESNUR 2012 conference paper credits Anka with a major role in spreading the vogue of extraterrestrial channeling, and treats him alongside the Seth material (Jane Roberts) that influenced him; a 2024 IPI Letters article compares Bashar's and Seth's cosmologies. In pop culture, Indian progressive-rock band Motherjane released a January 2015 single, “Clay Play,” based on Bashar — drummer John Thomas said the band found the discourse “very inspiring.”

One weak thread, labeled as such: a single book-summary site lists Shirley MacLaine as a “celebrity follower” in connection with Blueprint for Change — single weak source, treat as unverified. And no evidence was found that figures such as Joe Rogan cite Bashar; none is claimed here.

7 · What couldn't be verified

Printed plainly, because an honest gap beats a filled one:

  • Current private-session price — not published on bashar.org as of November 10, 2026, and the site says Anka is “not yet booking sessions for 2026.” The last firm figure is his own 2014 statement of $300/hour.
  • How far in the future Bashar lives — ~300 years (long-standing), ~700 years (Willa-related material), ~3,000 years (some 2026 coverage). Contested; no single official figure confirmed.
  • Exact YouTube subscriber count — sources conflict (~190K vs ~260K on the same analytics page).
  • Book sales in Japan — 300,000 vs “half a million” in different secondary sources; unverified.
  • Charitable giving — none found; absence of evidence in a web search is not proof none exists.
  • Lawsuits and complaints — none found in web search, including no BBB profile; a search-level negative, not a court-records search.
  • First Contact's year — documented premiere/release September 16, 2016; some filmographies list 2015.
  • The 1973 sighting details — entirely Anka's account; even the second sighting's distance is ~60 ft on his official page and ~70 ft in his 2014 interview.
  • Net worth — the only figure found (~$15M) is a skeptical critic's explicit speculation, not documentation.
